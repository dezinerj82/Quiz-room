Quiz Room Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 November 2026

Quiz Room is an educational quiz website hosted on GitHub Pages. This policy describes the information used when you sign in and take quizzes.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Quiz Room requests access to your Google account's basic profile and email address to identify the signed-in account. Google handles authentication. Quiz Room does not request your Google password.

Google Drive access

Quiz Room requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and access files it uses for quiz score reports in your Google Drive. The app creates or finds an app-managed quiz-score folder and uploads reports there. It does not request unrestricted access to all files in your Drive.

Quiz results and local data

Quiz Room processes the signed-in Google account's email address, quiz answers, and scores to show results and maintain progress. The website may save remembered account selection and quiz history in your browser's local storage. Quiz score reports may also be stored in your own Google Drive when authorized and when uploading succeeds. Browser-stored information remains on that browser until cleared.

Public score files

The website may display score reports from the public GitHub repository's score folder. Any reports intentionally committed to that public folder can be accessed by others. Do not publish personal or sensitive information in public score files.

Services involved

Google provides sign-in and Google Drive storage. GitHub Pages hosts the website and GitHub may serve public score files. Their respective privacy policies apply to their services. Quiz Room does not use the Google Drive permission to sell user data or for advertising.

Control and removal

You can revoke Quiz Room's access in your Google Account connections. You can delete app-created quiz reports from your Google Drive and clear this site's browser data to remove locally stored information. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already saved.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised for material updates.
