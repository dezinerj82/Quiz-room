Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Quiz Room is a learning website where you can take quizzes and save your results using your own Google account.

What information we use

  • Google sign-in: Quiz Room requests basic Google account permissions (including email and profile information) and uses your Google account ID to confirm your sign-in.
  • Quiz results: Completed quiz records include the subject, quiz name, score, total questions, question IDs, and start and completion times.
  • Google Drive: Quiz Room requests permission to work with the Drive files used by the app. It does not request permission to access all files in your Google Drive.

Where your data is stored

Quiz results are saved as JSON files in the Quiz Room / Scores folder in your own Google Drive. They are not published to the Quiz Room GitHub repository.

To keep you signed in, Quiz Room stores a temporary Google access token and its expiry time in your browser's local storage. Quiz scores may also be held in browser memory while the page is open. Quiz Room does not run a separate database for these records.

How your information is used

Quiz Room uses your Google account access to sign you in, save your completed quiz results, and show your quiz history and score summaries. The current website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts, and does not send quiz scores to advertisers.

Third-party services

Quiz Room uses Google for sign-in and Google Drive storage. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may process technical or account information under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Sign out: Use Log out in Quiz Room to remove its saved access token from this browser.
  • Delete results: Delete the saved score files or the Quiz Room folder from your Google Drive.
  • Remove access: You can revoke Quiz Room's Google access in your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as Quiz Room changes. The last-updated date above shows when this version was written.

Questions?

Contact the Quiz Room maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.
